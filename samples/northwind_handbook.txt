Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (fictional sample document)

Working hours
Core working hours are 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time. Outside core hours, employees may
choose when they work, as long as they complete 40 hours per week.

Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 6 unused days
can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March. Sick leave is
separate: employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year, and a medical certificate is required
for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval
from both the team lead and the HR department. Remote employees receive a one-time home office
allowance of INR 15,000.

Expenses
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip through the internal expense portal.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, books and conference
tickets. Requests above INR 10,000 need approval from the team lead before purchase.
